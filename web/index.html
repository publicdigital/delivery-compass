Before you rely on anything here — what this does and does not tell you
  • It quotes, it never interprets. Every requirement shown is the source’s own sentence, cited to the exact heading. There is no model writing summaries.
  • It covers a curated list of sources, not all government guidance. If a question returns nothing, that is a gap in this corpus — not evidence that no rule exists.
  • Conflict and supersession warnings are hand-curated and incomplete. The absence of a warning does not mean two documents agree. Unverified candidates are marked as such.
  • Dates are the publisher’s, not a currency guarantee. Every quote carries the date of the page it came from, deliberately even when recent. Confirm against the linked source.
  • It is a prototype. Tell me where it is wrong or useless — the form at the bottom of this page goes straight to whoever is building it.
